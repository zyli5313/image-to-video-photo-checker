Quick fixes

  • Blurry or tiny: re-export the original instead of a screenshot.
  • Cropped limbs: choose a wider shot, or keep the motion to the visible area (a head turn, a smile, hair in the wind).
  • Busy background: ask for a locked-off camera so the background does not warp.
